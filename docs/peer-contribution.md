# Voluntary resource-aware cooperation (M2)

`PeerContributionPolicy` is opt-in; default disabled, no optional content work or provider claims. Host supplies `HostConditions` (Wi-Fi/network available, charging); Rust does not infer battery or radio state. Unknown conditions fail required constraints. Policy includes Wi-Fi-only, charging-only, maximum authorized replica bytes/storage, bytes per session and participation duration. Effective limits are the minimum of policy and remaining sync budgets. Zero allowance or unmet constraint means skip, not fabricated participation.

Small M2 implementation may contribute by forwarding a bounded batch of **already owner-authorized** profile/photo replicas to reachable volunteers using existing PutProfile/PutPhoto and durable acknowledgements. Validate current signed profile, issuer/owner binding, manifest hash/length, original signed lease/expiry and all allocation/transfer caps before each transfer. A temporary session may perform bounded lease maintenance. Optional incoming serving/admission, parcel forwarding, autonomous repair and mobile provider announcements may remain explicitly planned; they are not required to claim this implemented finite outbound cooperation path.

Contribution input/cache allocation must fit max_storage_bytes, not merely expose a field; low-capacity tests must prevent uploads. Account state storage is separate from optional replica allocation. Per-session transferred bytes count actual shared transport usage plus policy-limited optional work. Do not download/retain arbitrary photos, extend lease expiry or override owner authorization. Acknowledgement only follows successful remote persistence; failed/partial replication reports exact confirmed count.

A batch contains at most one profile per owner; duplicate owners are rejected before optional transfer, so repeated idempotent acknowledgements cannot inflate the replica count. One confirmed replica means one selected peer acknowledged the profile and every photo in its manifest. A session that completes the mailbox scan but confirms only part of its requested contribution batch reports Partial, retaining its exact confirmed count; cancellation and exhausted mandatory budgets retain their stronger status.

When disabled, skipped or session closed, no optional PutProfile/PutPhoto, serving or storage writes occur. Background sync remains an outbound client unless an explicit bounded listener policy is implemented/tested. No phone claims durable availability. Close all session sockets at completion/cancellation; volunteers receiving valid replicas may retain them only under their normal leases/capacity. Peer participation can fail behind NAT or when no peer is reachable; data availability depends on reliable participating nodes.
